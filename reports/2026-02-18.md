**[42] [Bayesian inference in IV regressions](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3189~4bf2164061.en.pdf)**

- It is well known that standard frequentist inference breaks down in IV regressions with weak instruments.
- Bayesian inference with diffuse priors suffers from the same problem.
- We show that the issue arises because flat priors on the first-stage coefficients overstate instrument strength.
- In contrast, inference improves drastically when an uninformative prior is specified directly on the concentration parameter—the key nuisance parameter capturing instrument relevance.

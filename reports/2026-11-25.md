**[1] [The EBA examines the use stablecoins, including EMTs, in decentralised finance](https://www.eba.europa.eu/publications-and-media/press-releases/eba-examines-use-stablecoins-including-emts-decentralised-finance)**

- The European Banking Authority (EBA) today published a Report analysing the use of EMTs and other selected stablecoins in DeFi and the implications for consumers and supervisors.
- The EBA finds that stablecoins, including some EMTs issued under the Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA), play a central role in the DeFi ecosystem by providing liquidity, collateral and settlement in DeFi trading and lending activities.
- The Report also highlights consumer protection, and regulatory arbitrage risks arising from the growing interaction between stablecoins and DeFi.

**[1] [Decoding the cyber-AI nexus](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//press/financial-stability-publications/fsr/special/html/ecb.fsrart202611_02~8cca80a3c3.en.html)**

- The rapid evolution of artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping the cyber-threat landscape, with significant implications for financial stability in the euro area.
- This special feature explores the complex interplay between AI and cybersecurity, highlighting how advanced AI models are enabling attackers and defenders alike to enhance their capabilities.
- Highly capable and agentic AI systems are increasing the scale, speed and sophistication of attacks while also introducing new vulnerabilities.
- These include AI models themselves being exposed to novel types of attack and AI agents failing to act as their human operators intend.

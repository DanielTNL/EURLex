**[10] [A structural model of capital buffer usability](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3188~28f203e31a.en.pdf)**

- Under which conditions do usability constraints for regulatory capital buffers emerge?
- To answer this question, we build a non-linear structural banking sector model with a minimum capital requirement that banks are not allowed to breach, and a capital buffer requirement (CBR) that banks can breach but if they do so potential stigma applies.
- We prove that even very low stigma costs induce large buffer usability constraints, i.e.
- when faced with losses banks will deleverage significantly to avoid that their capital ratio falls below the CBR.

- The European Banking Authority (EBA) and the European Central Bank (ECB) today published the 2025 edition of their joint report on payment fraud.
- The report covers the semi-annual data for 2022 to 2024 and confirms that the legal requirement for strong customer authentication (SCA) introduced in 2020 has contributed to reducing fraud levels.
- However, it also highlights the need for continued vigilance and for security measures to be adapted to combat new emerging types of fraud.

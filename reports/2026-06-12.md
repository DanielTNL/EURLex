**[3] [​The EBA launches early consultation on simplified EU-wide stress test, with climate risk integration](https://www.eba.europa.eu/publications-and-media/press-releases/eba-launches-early-consultation-simplified-eu-wide-stress-test-climate-risk-integration)**

- The European Banking Authority (EBA) published today the draft methodology, templates, and template guidance for the 2027 EU-wide stress test.
- The 2027 exercise introduces significant simplifications to improve efficiency and risk sensitivity, while preserving the robustness and comparability of results.
- Key changes include a substantial reduction in data requirements, the alignment of information with harmonised supervisory reporting, and the integration of climate risks into the EU-wide stress test.
- A total of 63 banks from the EU and Norway, including 47 from the euro area, will participate, covering 75% of the EU banking sector.

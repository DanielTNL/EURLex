- ​The European Banking Authority (EBA) today launched a public consultation on draft Regulatory Technical Standards (RTS) specifying the operational risk management framework that institutions must have in place as per Article 323 of the Capital Requirements Regulation (CRR3).
- The draft RTS set out harmonised, proportionate requirements for the governance, management process and systems institutions should use to identify, assess, monitor and manage operational risk.
- The consultation runs until 31 December 2026.

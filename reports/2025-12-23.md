- Based on a series of novel experiments fielded within the ECB’s Consumer Expectations Survey, we provide evidence on the attitudes of euro area consumers towards a possible central bank digital currency (CBDC).
- We document substantial socio‑demographic heterogeneity in consumers’ awareness and willingness to adopt a CBDC.
- According to survey responses, a sizeable share of around 45% of households would be interested in potentially adopting this new asset.
- In a scenario where a CBDC is introduced in the euro area, consumers would allocate on average a moderate fraction of a positive wealth shock to such a new asset.

### CMU & Financial Markets

**[1] [The EBA responds to the Commission’s proposed changes to its draft technical standards on operational risk](https://www.eba.europa.eu/publications-and-media/press-releases/eba-responds-commissions-proposed-changes-its-draft-technical-standards-operational-risk)**

- The European Banking Authority (EBA) has today published an Opinion on the European Commission’s proposed amendments to the final draft Regulatory Technical Standards (RTS)[1],[2] specifying operational risk requirements under the Capital Requirements Regulation (CRR).
- It considers that two amendments proposed by the Commission could affect the consistency, transparency and supervisory effectiveness of capital requirements for operational risk.

**[2] [EBA Report shows persistent gender imbalance and pay gaps in EU banking leadership](https://www.eba.europa.eu/publications-and-media/press-releases/eba-report-shows-persistent-gender-imbalance-and-pay-gaps-eu-banking-leadership)**

- The European Banking Authority (EBA) publishes today the results of its benchmarking analysis&nbsp;on diversity practices in the management bodies of more than 850 institutions credit institutions and investment firms across the European Union (EU).
- As of 31 December 2024, significant gender imbalances and pay gaps persisted, particularly at senior management level, despite some progress compared with the situation in 2021.
- The EBA calls on institutions to consider promoting a more balanced representation of genders, and on competent authorities to continue assessing diversity and gender pay gap practices as part of their supervisory reviews.

**[6] [A least-squares filter for sequence-space models](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3191~8bac225c62.en.pdf)**

- Sequence-space models are becoming increasingly popular in macroeconomics, especially in the heterogeneous-agent literature.
- However, the econometric toolkit for users of these models remains less developed than that available for traditional state-space methods.
- This note introduces an algorithm for efficiently filtering unobserved shocks in linear sequence-space models.
- The proposed filter solves a least-squares optimization problem in closed form and returns the expectation of unobserved shocks conditional on observed data.

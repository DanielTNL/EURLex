**[45] [Beat the heat, the role of heat waves and droughts in regional EU economies](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3248~f871c3bd85.en.pdf)**

- Europe is increasingly exposed to heat waves and droughts, but their short-term economic effects across sectors remain hard to predict.
- This study develops climate-augmented models to predict real growth in per capita value added across 1,117 EU regions (2002–2022), by combining economic indicators with high-frequency climate data.
- When using machine learning (ML, Random Forest and XGBoost), climate variables improve predictions in agriculture, while gains for other sectors are limited and do not outperform economic models.
- Heat wave indicators consistently enhance predictive performance, whereas drought effects vary by sector.

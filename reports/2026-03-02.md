**[34] [Bond funds’ risk taking and monetary policy](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3196~d1ad5a2aab.en.pdf)**

- Using granular security-level data from bond funds domiciled in the US and the euro area, we identify a market-based risk-taking channel of monetary policy transmission via the credit-risk and the maturity structure of bond funds’ portfolios.
- We measure credit risk at the fund level as the weighted average credit rating of the fund’s bond holdings.
- We find that accommodative monetary policies by the Fed and the ECB are associated with increased risk in bond funds’ portfolios.
- Interestingly, risk-taking is more pronounced for funds with longer-term holdings relative to short-term ones and unconventional monetary policy exerts stronger market-based risk-taking effects than interest rate policy.

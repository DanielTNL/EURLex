      of the EU Treaties . It also provides for participation in the EU programmes such as those for research

      likely to affect common rules or alter their scope. The EU’s external action is set out in Title V of the Treaty on the European Union and in Part 5 of the Treaty
      on the Functioning of the European Union (TFEU). The procedure for negotiating and
      adopting international agreements is set out in Articles 207 and 218 of the TFEU for the common commercial policy and for the other external action areas,
      respectively.

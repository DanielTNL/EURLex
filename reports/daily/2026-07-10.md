A. Political context, problem definition and subsidiarity check
Political context
The European Maritime Fisheries and Aquaculture Fund (EMFAF) established under Regulation (EU) 2021/1139 channels funding from the Union budget to support the Common Fisheries Policy (CFP), the European Union’s maritime policy and the Union’s international commitments in the field of ocean governance. The conservation of marine resources, being an EU exclusive competence, puts a responsibility on the Union in terms of policy making and financing. The ‘simpler and faster Europe’ communication 1 puts forward a new way of working between EU institutions, Member States and stakeholders in the spirit of partnership and cooperation, to simplify EU rules, reduce regulatory burden and improve rules’ design and implementation. This includes systematic simplification efforts to streamline regulatory burden, including decision-making procedures for rendering access to EU funds simpler, faster, and lighter. Problem the initiative aims to tackle
Despite its critical objectives, the current regulatory framework is posing challenges to Member States in terms of effective implementation. The midterm EMFAF evaluation and feedback gathered regularly from Member States, stakeholders, Advisory Councils etc, show that in the middle of the programming period, the EMFAF funding opportunities are considered limited and lack flexibility. The need to ensure legal clarity and reduce legal complexity is identified as the main barrier for supporting more effectively the energy transition ambition, small-scale fisheries’ fleet and generational renewal in the sector. In essence, national authorities and the representatives of the sector consider that certain EMFAF rules constitute blocking elements to unlock investments.

**[8] [The macroeconomic effects of defence spending: what impact beyond GDP?](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3310~6ef5699d9a.en.pdf)**

- This paper estimates the macroeconomic effects of defence spending for a panel of up to 27 EU member states over 1999–2025 using Panel Local Projections.
- We look beyond the output multiplier that dominates the literature to trace the price implications of defence spending and the demand-side channels through which effects operate.
- A key methodological contribution is a country-specific order-date adjustment for capital expenditure, constructed from the SIPRI Arms Transfers Database, which shifts the estimated (unitary) peak GDP multiplier from around year one to approximately year three to four.
- Under this specification, the HICP inflation response is higher and occurs earlier.

- Central clearing counterparties (CCPs) manage counterparty risk by requiring clearing members to post margins.
- This paper explores the role of margins as “canaries in the coal mine:” By inducing defaults of fragile counterparties before contract maturity, margin calls enable CCPs to transfer these contracts to other counterparties, thereby preserving risk sharing.
- Our model reveals a pecking order of CCP risk management tools.
- When fragility is low, loss sharing among original counterparties suffices.

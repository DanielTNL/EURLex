**[34] [Fiscal integration as a Pareto improvement](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3297~601502aed4.en.pdf)**

- Fiscal integration in a currency union is generally understood as a transfer from fiscally strong to fiscally weak countries.
- I show that this changes when common debt is paired with a non-defaultable common tax, even if national debt remains defaultable.
- The risky country gains from intertemporal trade: it receives a grant financed by a perpetual obligation it discounts more heavily than lenders do.
- The cost to the safe member is second-order and the tax removes it by insuring imperfectly correlated cycles rather than transferring resources in expectation.

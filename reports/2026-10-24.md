**[4] [Headwind or tailwind? How banks interpret borrowers’ AI exposure](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3302~7ff8a286bc.en.pdf)**

- General-purpose technologies create large opportunities but make their distribution across firms difficult to assess in real time.
- We use banks as interpreters of the rise of generative AI by studying lender-specific probability-of-default (PD) estimates for private firms in four large euro-area economies.
- As public attention to AI rises after ChatGPT, banks lower assessed default risk for borrowers in highly AI-exposed industries by about 5%.
- The reassessment is weaker for labor-intensive firms and stronger among lenders with pre-existing industry expertise.

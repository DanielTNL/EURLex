**[26] [European temporary migration in a two country DSGE model](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3226~cde6568696.en.pdf)**

- Free movement of labour across borders can influence business cycle dynamics in the affected countries.
- This paper studies the macroeconomic implications of temporary migration using a two-country dynamic stochastic general equilibrium model calibrated to represent the “old” EU Member States (EU15) and the “new” Member States (NMS12).
- The model introduces fully endogenous temporary migration and combines it with search-and-matching frictions in labour markets.
- Workers migrate temporarily in response to differences in labour market conditions and wages, allowing productivity shocks to affect local labour supply.

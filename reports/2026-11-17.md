**[1] [Monetary policy, segmented capital markets and innovation](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3311~66cc2a4d65.en.pdf)**

- We identify a novel mechanism linking monetary policy, innovation, and capital-market segmentation.
- When capital markets are imperfectly integrated, contractionary monetary policy tightens the financing constraints of externally financed firms, amplifying the decline in innovation induced by tighter financial conditions; monetary easing operates in reverse.
- Using granular euro-area firm-level data on balance sheets and patenting, matched with bank-level and credit-registry information, we show that monetary policy affects innovation through financing constraints.
- The effects are strongest for firms with high intangible intensity and for firms operating in AI-related sectors.

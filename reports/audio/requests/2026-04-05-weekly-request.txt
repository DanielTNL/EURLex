The statutory audit serves as a legally mandated review of financial records, providing stakeholders—including investors and shareholders—with an assurance of the accuracy of financial statements. ### Why It Matters The importance of statutory audits cannot be overstated. This is particularly relevant for public-interest entities (PIEs), which are subject to stringent audit requirements under Regulation No 537/2014.

- The European Banking Authority (EBA) today outlines the way forward for the finalisation of proposals to simplify EU supervisory reporting, following extensive dialogue with the banking industry and feedback received during the public consultation.
- The package that is currently being finalised will introduce additional simplification measures, greater proportionality and extended implementation timelines, reducing reporting costs while preserving the information necessary for effective supervision.
- The final report on the draft Implementing Technical Standards (ITS) on supervisory reporting will be published by the EBA in December 2026.

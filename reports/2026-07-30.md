**[1] [A SPOT in the dark: using AI to assess financial stability risks](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3262~7169a94638.en.pdf)**

- Financial stability risks consist of two distinct components: vulnerabilities and possible trigger events.
- While there has been considerable progress regarding the measurement of vulnerabilities, the assessment of possible trigger events remains largely qualitative.
- To fill this gap, we employ Large Language Models to extract information about the Severity and Probability Of potential Trigger events (SPOT) from a large dataset of financial news articles over the period2005 – 2026.
- The SPOT indicator increases ahead of major historical trigger events, correctly identifies trigger sources, and helps to improve forward looking model estimates of downside risks to the economy.

**[5] [Financial integration and the transmission of monetary policy in the euro area](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3165~0b3b657c29.en.pdf)**

- We study how financial integration shapes the transmission of monetary policy to consumer prices and output in the euro area.
- Using local projections, we document that the effect of financial integration is continuous: greater integration systematically strengthens the pass-through of monetary policy.
- When integration falls to low levels—around the first quartile of its historical distribution— transmission to both prices and output becomes statistically and economically insignificant.
- The amplification pattern is pervasive across member states and more pronounced in peripheral economies.

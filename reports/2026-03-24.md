**[8] [Interlinking payment systems and trade flows](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3202~028f70f8ec.en.pdf)**

- This paper provides the first causal estimate of the economic impact of interlinking payment systems across countries.
- We exploit a new dataset of payment systems interlinking initiatives, which identifies over 2,000 connections, and employ standard gravity methods to estimate their impact on trade flows.
- Consistent with trade costs theory, we find that inter-connected countries have around 4% higher trade volumes, roughly half the effect of a trade agreement and a quarter of the effect of a common currency area.
- Our results isolate the average effect on trade, of directly connecting fast payment systems, net of country pairs already accessing the correspondent banking network.

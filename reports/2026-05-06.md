**[50] [Sectoral interconnectedness in the euro area economies: insights from network analysis](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3223~de6dfd90a1.en.pdf)**

- Using who-to-whom data for the last quarter of 2024, I build networks of financial interconnections in the euro area countries.
- After representing them in chord diagrams, I consider centrality metrics and find that banks dominate, with four exceptions: Cyprus, Ireland, Luxembourg and Malta.
- In these countries, other financial institutions and investment funds are at the core, with limited links to domestic sectors and strong ones with the rest of the world.
- A comparison across countries reveals substantial homogeneity between networks in the sixteen euro area countries and large differences with Cyprus, Ireland, Luxembourg and Malta.

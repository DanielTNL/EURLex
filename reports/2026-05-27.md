**[25] [Employment effects of EU-ETS prices](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3237~617a2b8de5.en.pdf)**

- This paper studies the employment effects of carbon pricing under the European Union’s Emissions Trading System (EU-ETS).

**[41] [Let the tree decide: FABART. A non-parametric factor model for nonlinear oil shock transmission](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3265~cde079e4dc.en.pdf)**

- The question of how oil supply news shocks transmit to real activity, financial conditions, and regional labor markets is back at the center of the macroeconomic research agenda.
- To answer this question, we introduce the Factor Bayesian Additive Regression Tree (FABART) model, a nonlinear factor-augmented vector autoregression model, and apply it to a large U.S.
- macro-financial dataset with externally identified oil supply news shocks.
- The framework combines a large macro-financial information set with a flexible nonparametric measurement equation, allowing nonlinear transmission to emerge from the data rather than being imposed through a pre-specified functional form.
